Wings of the Promise

Fly the dove from the storm into the sunrise

How to play

Tap anywhere or press Space to flap the dove's wings. Gravity is always pulling you toward the waters below.

Glide through the gaps between the stone pillars — each pillar passed is +1 point.

🌿 Catch olive branches floating in the gaps for +2 points. Catch them back-to-back to build an olive streak.

📖 Every so often a Scripture verse unlocks and the journey enters a new chapter — the sky itself changes as the story moves from flood to promise.

⏸ Pause anytime with the button in the corner (or Esc).

The flight is endless — the question is how far your wings of faith will carry you. Speed rises and gaps tighten the further you fly.
